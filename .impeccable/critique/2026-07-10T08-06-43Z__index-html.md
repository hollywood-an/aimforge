## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Borderline pass. Real named identity (Midnight Range, Earned Light, tonal three-layer elevation) and disciplined overlays. Four tells at the front door: (1) full-color 📈 emoji on the Stats button — the only out-of-palette pixels, classic emoji-as-icon; (2) 14 structurally identical scenario cards in a uniform grid; (3) palette is the first-order category reflex ("aim trainer → near-black + cyan + glow"); (4) glow leaked past the Earned Light whitelist (HUD score shadow, cyan hover box-shadow, ambient radial wash on every overlay).

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs` exit 2 — one finding, `em-dash-overuse` (5, file-level). Mostly false positives (title, keybinding separators, a placeholder glyph); only the stats empty-state copy is genuine prose.

**Measured evidence:** Contrast — one real fail: `#menu-benchmark .btn-sub` at 4.09:1 (needs 4.5). All other sampled text passes AA (the `#8892a6` dim body sits 5.4–5.8:1 on panels). Focus — zero author `:focus`/`:focus-visible` in CSS; only the Chrome default dark-blue UA ring shows on a near-black theme. Motion — zero `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks; the only infinite animation is the results PB badge pulse; no screen transitions. Load — Google Fonts CSS is render-blocking; `three.module.js` is 1.27 MB served uncompressed (87.7% of page weight).

## Priority Issues

**[P1] HUD readouts wash out against the arena.** Dense cyan gridlines (opacity 0.35/0.12) blend into a light blue-gray field at gameplay res; white timer + glowing cyan score + dim FPS all fight the bright wall. The sacred surface is the least legible one. Fix: drop grid opacity to ~0.12/0.04 (engine.js visual constants), dark scrim behind HUD text instead of cyan glow.

**[P1] Accessibility promises unimplemented.** PRODUCT.md commits to reduced-motion + visible focus; neither exists. `blur()` on every click dumps keyboard focus to `<body>`. Toast has no `aria-live`; canvases unlabeled. Fix: reduced-motion block, designed cyan focus-visible ring, mouse-only blur guard + focus-new-panel, aria-live toast, canvas labels, main landmark, aria-pressed tabs.

**[P1] The two sanctioned peaks are static.** Results score and benchmark rank appear via bare `textContent` while the PB badge pulses infinitely — the celebration budget is inverted. Fix: score count-up (~700ms ease-out-expo), tier/delta land after; rank-reveal beat + staggered stage rows; cap PB pulse at 3; all with reduced-motion fallbacks.

**[P1] No first-run guidance.** Fresh profile: 14 equal cards, no recommended start, and the loudest CTA (BENCHMARK) is the most punishing first click. 14 gold "Not played yet" labels read as nagging. Fix: "Start here: Gridshot" affordance when empty, always-visible bench explainer, neutral no-PB state.

**[P1] Benchmark abandon destroys progress silently.** Esc / Q / "Abandon benchmark" discard all completed stages with no confirm, at the emotional low point. Fix: in-panel confirm ("Abandon benchmark? 3 of 6 done").

**[P2] Emoji-as-icons.** 📈 (color) mixed with ⚙ ⬡ ▶ ↻ ← (dingbats). Replace with one inline-SVG family, currentColor, uniform stroke.

**[P2] Menu structure.** 14 identical cards; redundant category chip repeated under active filter; R/Esc hints below fold; cards clip mid-title with no scroll affordance.

**[P2] System breaks its own rules.** Orbitron 6× on Settings (limit 2); off-whitelist glow (HUD score/message); card hover box-shadow; ambient radial cyan wash + backdrop-blur on `#screens`.

**[P2] Benchmark trust gap.** "4.17 / 7 points" never explained; tier thresholds unknowable; no "stage N of 6" on results; bench table lacks headers.

**[P2] Custom drill is a 9-decision wall.** Ungrouped unitless spinners, no presets, no reset. Chunk into 3 labeled groups, add presets (Flick/Track/Micro) + reset, Spread hint, sliders.

**[P2] Settings destructive actions.** Reset executes instantly (its neighbor confirms — a trap); delete-all uses off-brand native confirm with zero post-action feedback. Add on-brand confirms + success toast.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** 14 equal cards, no "start here"; unitless "Spread"/"Lifetime"; benchmark math unexplained; bench rules hidden after first run; off-brand native confirm.

**Sam (a11y):** zero reduced-motion; no focus-visible; blur()-to-body; toast not announced; bare-canvas graphs with no text alt; card buttons with block innerHTML.
